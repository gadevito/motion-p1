## Part B — what do sensors buy for the segments without one?

Part B reads in three steps: whether a learned graph model gains from its graph, as the spacing of the network
(E11) and the instrumented fraction (E9) change; whether the labels carry a spatial signal that predictors
without training can use, and what remains of it once calibrated and once the calendar is known (E12, E12-bis,
E12-ter); and how close each placement rule brings the uncovered segments to a sensor (E10-geo).
