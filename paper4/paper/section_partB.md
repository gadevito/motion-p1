## Part B — what do sensors buy for the segments without one?

Part B reads in four steps. First, whether a learned graph model gains from its graph, as the spacing of the
network (E11) and the instrumented fraction (E9) change. Second, whether the labels carry a spatial signal at
all (E12). Third, what that signal is worth without a model: calibrated on the instrumented segments (E12-bis)
and net of the calendar (E12-ter). Fourth, how close each placement rule brings the uncovered segments to a
sensor (E10-geo).
