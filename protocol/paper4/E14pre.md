## What runs (`e14.py pre`, once; the command refuses to run if its report exists)

**C0.1 — hidden-node invariance, eight trajectories of 50 updates (400 updates).** Madrid, seed 0, configuration C1, initialisation stream `p4b.K_INIT`. For **each of the four cell types** (S0-full, S0-nodyn, S1-full, S1-nodyn): one trajectory on the ordinary setup and one on the perturbed setup (`_perturbed_copy`: the factor readings of every validation-hidden and test-hidden node replaced by noise on the input side, flow and occupancy likewise; the target series of the test-hidden nodes replaced; validation labels kept). The comparison is **per cell type, original against perturbed**: the 50 per-step training losses, the parameters after 50 updates and the validation predictions on 64 origins must be identical to the bit; the factor scaler and the method's graph must be identical between the two setups. Full against nodyn, or S0 against S1, is not an invariance check and is not compared here. Criterion: pass if the identities hold for the four cell types, recorded as four explicit verdicts and a summary verdict in the report; otherwise E14 is not signed until the cause is found and corrected (dated note, new tag of this protocol), and **no budget decision can be taken** (`e14.py budget` refuses a report whose C0.1 is not passed, incomplete, or produced by other code). CUDA kernels are requested in deterministic mode where a deterministic implementation exists (`torch.use_deterministic_algorithms(True, warn_only=True)`, cuBLAS workspace fixed); whether the result is bit-identical is what C0.1 and the benchmark repetitions measure.

**Benchmark — seconds per update and per validation, four cell types.** Same setup; 60 updates per repetition, three repetitions per cell type, checkpoint every 10 updates; seconds per update = (t₆₀ − t₁₀ − Σ of the five validation passes between them)/50 per repetition, **net of the validation passes**, median of three (the gross value (t₆₀ − t₁₀)/50 of E13-pre is recorded alongside, not used); seconds per validation = the median duration of a validation pass (all validation origins, inputs = observed nodes, validation-hidden targets); the budget rule counts each once (U_max × s/update + 24 × s/validation); peak device memory recorded; the three repetitions are identical runs and their final parameters are compared (reported as a determinism observation, no criterion). The medians per cell type enter the budget rule of `E14.md` / ADAPTER_SPEC §9 through `e14.py budget --h-cal H --by NAME`, after the responsible author has stated H_cal (20 % reserve included) **before** this benchmark: H_cal ______ h, stated on ______.

Total: 400 + 720 = **1 120 updates**, four cell types, one city, one seed; no checkpoint selection, no test node, no real-data result.
