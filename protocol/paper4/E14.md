## Question

On the regression task (continuous factor r at the hidden sites, four horizons, MAE), with the dedicated model MoGERNN adapted to the E11 perimeter (schema S0 as signed in P4R §6: the observed sites' **factor history** as the only dynamic reading, calendar, topology, explicit masks; no flow, no occupancy, no historical average, no coordinates, no target-specific embeddings; teacher forcing disabled in every arm):

- **(H2.1.c)** does the dedicated model on S0 beat the calendar baseline (per-slot median of observed-training sites) at the hidden sites, per city?
- **(H2.2.c)** dynamic benefit **without** coordinates: full S0 against no-dynamic S0, per city;
- **(H2.3.c)** dynamic benefit **with** coordinates: full S1 against no-dynamic S1 (S1 = S0 + coordinates with a shared encoding), per city;
- **(H2.4.c)** the interaction: (H2.2.c) − (H2.3.c), per city — the **difference between the two dynamic benefits**: positive means the benefit of the factor history is larger without coordinates, negative larger with coordinates; it is not a statement about the benefit or detriment of the coordinates themselves.

Twelve confirmatory contrasts, the members of family F2 (P4R_REV1 §2), read on the sign only through the simultaneous band at level 0.98. The effect of coordinates at fixed dynamics (static benefit) is secondary and descriptive. No comparison with G0-reg or G1-reg (suspended).

## Arms and cells (C1)

| Factor | Levels |
| --- | --- |
| City | Madrid, London, Melbourne (as E9) |
| Seed (outer split) | 0–4 (node sets identical to E9's at f = 0.20) |
| Cell type | **S0-full** (dynamic readings on, no coordinates); **S0-nodyn** (dynamic suppression); **S1-full** (S0 + coordinates); **S1-nodyn** |

**60 cells**, 300 fits (4 development configurations + 1 final per cell, nested tuning per split as E13/E13B, adequacy rule, extension, lock, one development run per cell). The calendar baseline is a table (no fit): per-slot (weekday × 15-min slot, 672 slots) median of the observed-training sites' factor at the target time, global observed-training median as fallback for slots with fewer than 5 values; the mean variant for RMSE, secondary; it uses no value of any hidden node.

**Dynamic suppression** (P4R §6; ADAPTER_SPEC §3): the factor history set to zero for every node and step (`r_in` ≡ 0); the availability mask, the node-input indicator, calendar, coordinates (S1), graph, targets and target masks are **identical** to the corresponding full cell (asserted by C0.3), so that the contrast concerns the factor history only; no assumption is made on the completion's output. **S1 coordinates**: UTM coordinates standardised on observed-training sites, entered as two exogenous channels of the encoder and decoder cells (ADAPTER_SPEC §3), identical in the two S1 cells. Information availability as E11: a masked or hidden node contributes nothing of its own in training, selection or inference; hidden-node rules as E13B (own readings never in inputs or preprocessing statistics; calendar, masks and coordinates admitted on hidden nodes).

## Adapter

`protocol/paper4/rafforzamento/ADAPTER_SPEC_E14.md` is binding. In one paragraph: the experts' completion is kept single-channel on the factor and driven by the explicit masks (unknown nodes from the mask, replacement where the reading is missing or the node is not an input); the DCRNN encoder (2 layers, hidden 64, k_hop 1) and the autoregressive decoder (2 layers as in the original `Seq2Seq`, `fc 64 → 1`, first input = the completed last step, teacher forcing disabled) are the original cells with the calendar, the mask and — in S1 — the coordinates concatenated as exogenous input channels (the original has no entry point for them); MSE on standardised values over the target entries of all 8 decoder steps as the training loss; a factor scaler on the observed-training valid values, inverted before MAE/RMSE; the method's own graph (Gaussian kernel on road-network distance within a declared common road radius of 2 km applied before σ, σ = std of the distances, threshold 0.1) on the E11 aligned graph, built from topology only, random walks cached; no dropout (none in the original); AdamW 1e-3 / 1e-4 without scheduler as the reference configuration (the original script disables it), plateau as a declared adaptation in C3 with a fixed policy. The parameter count of the adapter is recorded by the preflight (the original's 226 270 belongs to the original configuration).

## C0 — correctness checks

**C0.1 Hidden-node invariance** (under `E14pre.md`): for each of the four cell types, one trajectory on the ordinary setup and one on the perturbed setup (readings of hidden nodes replaced by noise; test labels replaced; validation labels kept): training losses, parameters and validation predictions identical to the bit, per cell type — **eight trajectories of 50 updates (400 updates)**. Also asserted: the factor scaler and the graph are identical between the two setups.

**C0.2 Synthetic regression targets with a known dependence, on realisations not yet observed (after the tag, before C1).** Fully specified in ADAPTER_SPEC §7. Madrid seed 0; `e13.synth_processes` with **seeds 20261121 (dependent) and 20261122 (independent)**, the latent factor z as the continuous target; one realisation per condition, no substitution. **Both** realisations validated before the first fit with the privileged MAE diagnostic (median tables by weekly slot, 672 slots; neighbourhood feature = input nodes' z propagated by Σ_{k≤14}(A_q)^k over the propagated mass, 32 bins plus the unreachable bin; fallbacks at 5 values; mean over the four horizons): dependent gain **≥ 10 %**, independent **within ±1 %**, thresholds fixed now; a realisation outside them is recorded as invalid and nothing is trained. Then **S0-full against S0-nodyn** per realisation — **four trainings of 2 000 updates** — configuration C1, initialisation stream 7 (the development stream, so that the initialisation state of §C0.5b is the stream-7 initialisation), validation every 250; best checkpoint = checkpoint ≥ 500 with minimal validation MAE of S0-full; V at that checkpoint = the mean over the four horizons of the per-horizon ratios 100·(MAE_nodyn,h − MAE_full,h)/MAE_nodyn,h (the aggregation of the primary contrasts), the checkpoint being selected on the mean MAE. Dependent **passes if V ≥ +3 %**; otherwise C1 does not start (no acceptance route). A control whose validation MAE or contrast is not finite at some checkpoint ≥ 500 is **undefined**: not a pass, no acceptance route, procedure stopped. Independent: **anomaly if V > +3 % at any checkpoint ≥ 500**, explained in a dated note and accepted with an acceptance limited to that anomaly, bound to the report fingerprint and frozen tag; reported if V < −3 % at some checkpoint; not a demonstration that an advantage is impossible.

**C0.3 Schema parity and adapter equivalence (preflight, no update).** Equivalence of the adapter with the original on the original configuration (single channel, no exogenous channels, batch-sum unknown detection, teacher forcing as coded): forward identical within 1e-6 on a synthetic batch to the original `Model`; isolated node leaves the others identical; consistent permutation permutes the outputs; evaluation state unchanged; cached random walks equal to the recomputed ones. Then, in the mask-driven mode, on a training batch and a validation batch: the three mask notions distinct (node admitted as input, missing reading, node to complete); S0-nodyn and S1-nodyn with **only `r_in` exactly zero** and every other tensor (`m_in`, `node_in`, calendar, coordinates, graph, targets, target masks) identical to the corresponding full cell; S1 cells differing from S0 cells only in the coordinate channels; coordinates of hidden nodes true and not altering any channel of observed nodes; masks binary; no inference of sensor presence from numeric values (a non-input node whose series is set to a non-zero constant yields identical inputs for every other node and identical masks); scaler statistics and graph unchanged by any perturbation of hidden nodes' series.

**C0.4 London files** differ from Madrid's (re-asserted).

**C0.5 Validation/test disjointness; reachability and sensitivity (ADAPTER_SPEC §6).** No configuration or checkpoint choice for split s reads any node of test_s (static check of the development, extension and final code). **(a) Structural dependency, before the signature, all 15 (city, seed), no trained state:** boolean dependency propagation through the operations of the adapter (completion where `m_in` = 0 only — non-input nodes, and input nodes with a missing reading in the period —, gates with the reset feeding the candidate so that two hops compose per cell once the hidden state is non-zero and one hop at a layer's first step, two layers, recurrent steps, decoder re-entry) gives, per horizon, the targets whose dependence on at least one input node is **not excluded** — an upper bound of the architecture's information path on 0/1 patterns: an empty row excludes a dependence, a non-empty row does not demonstrate one; reported as the share of validation-hidden, test-hidden and sampled training targets (20 masks) with a non-empty dependency row and the row sizes; hop distances to the nearest input node on `A ∪ Aᵀ` and shares within the declared radii 14 and 21 as descriptive measures; the reference linear mass Σ_{k≤14}(A_q)^k over the input nodes, as I3 did, clearly distinct. **(b) Local numerical sensitivity, after the tag, only on the C0.2 state:** Madrid seed 0, dependent condition, arm S0-full, at its best checkpoint and at the stream-7 initialisation: the gradient of each decoder output with respect to each input node's series in double precision, reported as the share of hidden targets with |gradient| > 10⁻¹² and its distribution — a property of weights, activations and inputs, not a reachability classification. Neither (a) nor (b) is a validity criterion; the share of training targets with a non-empty dependency row is reported next to every contrast, with the bound's nature stated.

## Training, selection, adequacy

As E13B (configurations C1–C4 of the family-specific grid of ADAPTER_SPEC §5 — C1 the original training script's settings, AdamW 1e-3 / weight decay 1e-4 / no scheduler; C2 learning rate 3e-4; C3 plateau scheduler as a declared adaptation with the policy of ADAPTER_SPEC §5 (step after each validation checkpoint, never at 0, evaluate → step → save); C4 weight decay 0; **no dropout**, the original model has none — fixed here and not tuned further; reduced grid, if the budget rule applies: C1 and C2; checkpoints every 500, selection on validation-hidden MAE per split, development stream 7 and final stream 6, adequacy rule — minimum not at the cap, last improvement ≤ 0.5 % — extension of a (city, cell type) group to 2·U_max with reproduction within 1e-9, global lock `e14_adequacy.json`, one development run per cell with the deviation rule). **U_max = 12 000** (a choice of this protocol, of the order of the original's shortest trainings; ADAPTER_SPEC §9), extension to 24 000; the budget rule (ADAPTER_SPEC §9): the responsible author states the Spark calendar H_cal, including a 20 % reserve for extensions and reproductions, before E14-pre; after it, the cost in hours = Σ over the four cell types of n_fits × (12 000 × s_per_update + 24 × s_per_validation)/3600 with n_fits = 75 per cell type (full grid) or 45 (reduced); if the full grid exceeds 0.8·H_cal, the uniform reduction of P4R §8 (**C1 and C2**, 180 fits) is applied and recorded before the tag, provided it fits 0.8·H_cal; if the reduced-grid cost exceeds 0.8·H_cal, the scope is renegotiated collegially; U_max is not lowered to fit the calendar.

## Unit of analysis and inference

Cell = (city, seed, corridor block, node, origin). MAE on test-hidden nodes per horizon in factor units (scaler inverted), mean over the four horizons; RMSE secondary. Contrasts per city as paired differences on identical targets, origins and masks, each per-split value being the mean over the four horizons of the per-horizon MAE ratio, the city value the mean over the five splits: H2.1.c = 100·(MAE_cal − MAE_S0full)/MAE_cal; H2.2.c = 100·(MAE_S0nodyn − MAE_S0full)/MAE_S0nodyn; H2.3.c likewise in S1; H2.4.c = H2.2.c − H2.3.c (points). Two-way block bootstrap (grid cells × ISO weeks) with multiplicities shared across splits and cell types, 4 000 replicates, generator `default_rng([20261120, city index])`; undefined replicates handled as E13B; the **F2 band**: sup-t simultaneous band over the twelve contrasts at level 0.98, from which every confirmatory statement is derived — for H2.1–H2.3: lower bound > 0 = benefit, upper bound < 0 = detriment, otherwise undetermined; for H2.4: lower bound > 0 = the dynamic benefit is larger without coordinates, upper bound < 0 = larger with coordinates, otherwise undetermined; `e14.py analysis` reports marginal intervals and unadjusted p-values with the PROVISIONAL label; the F2 statements are produced by the campaign-wide analysis. No equivalence or negligible-benefit statement (no regression margin). Secondary, descriptive: static benefit at fixed dynamics per city; test-hidden nodes with dependency possible / excluded (the C0.5(a) bound at horizon 1, test period); selected configurations; RMSE; per-horizon values.

## What invalidates the experiment

Everything listed in E9, E11, E13B, plus: a model update on a backend other than the Spark CUDA assignment (every training command refuses it; the deterministic preflight, the structural measures and the float64 sensitivity diagnostic are the only CPU computations, declared); the adapter, schema, masks, loss or training policy changed after the signature; teacher forcing enabled in any arm; a comparison with G0-reg/G1-reg presented as a result; the planned reuse of S0-full as the A2 fit without the identity of schema, masks, loss and training policy asserted in C0.3; the synthetic seeds of C0.2 changed.
