## 1. What happened

1. **A0.2 (synthetic positive control of E13) failed twice.** The control asks that G1 (spatial exchange on) beat G0 (off) by ≥ 3 points of weighted cross-entropy on validation-hidden nodes when the synthetic target process is dependent along the graph. Control version 1: V(G1) = +0.27. After a declared post-hoc revision of the control (version 2, with a tabular diagnostic of +12.6 points on the graph-accessible feature): V(G1) = +0.41. Both reports kept (`e13_a0_synth_v1_FAILED.json`, `e13_a0_synth.json`). A1 is blocked by the protocol's own rule; the cause of the failures is not established.
2. **I1 — larger budget.** 10 000 updates instead of 2 000: V(G1) = +0.28 at the best checkpoint; both arms flat (validation wCE 1.055–1.070, training loss 1.033–1.045) over the whole run. Outcome: failure still unexplained.
3. **I2 — where the information is lost (three conditions, four trainings, 2 000 updates).** R1, G0 with the targets' own dynamic readings restored: +52.2 (threshold +10 reached). R2, G0 plus an explicit input channel carrying the observed neighbours' factor propagated along the G1 adjacency within 6 steps and divided by the propagated mass (history-free, no graph convolution): +15.6 (threshold +3 reached). R3, G1 on a control with greater spatial regularity (bandwidth 3 000 m; tabular diagnostic +29.6): +1.2 (threshold +3 not reached). Prudent reading: the network uses dynamic readings and learns from a normalised neighbourhood feature handed to it as an input; through the graph convolutions of `p4b.GWN` the advantage stayed below threshold even on a highly regular field.
4. **I3 — structural measurement, no training, no target series loaded** (`e13_a0_struct.py`, log and json committed, `03c9ee1`). For the three cities and five seeds at f = 0.20: hop distance of masked nodes to the nearest observed node on the adjacency G1 receives, and mass assigned to them by the reference propagation operator Σ_{k≤h} A^k from the input nodes, h = 6 (receptive field of the predictions: 3 graph convolutions of order 2), 8, 12.

Adjacency: `p4b.kernel_adjacency` — Gaussian kernel on edge length, identity on the diagonal, symmetric normalisation; not identity-dominated (off-diagonal weights mean 0.17–0.19, neighbours' share of row mass 0.55–0.59). Observed nodes at f = 0.20: 102–194 per (city, seed).

## 3. What this does and does not establish

- It establishes a **property of the frozen design** (whole-cell masking at f = 0.20, the measured edge-length distributions, kernel adjacency, 6-step receptive field of the predictions), to be kept in its two parts: a consistent share of the nodes G1 must predict — validation-hidden, test-hidden and sampled training targets alike — has **no path** from an observed node within six steps (shares per city and group in the table above and in `e13_a0_struct.log`), and many of the others receive a **small mass** in the reference propagation operator (median ≤ 10⁻³, against ≈ 5.6 for an observed node). Reachability and mass are different things and are reported separately.
- It does **not** establish that the actual inputs of G0 and G1 differ little, nor how much of what reaches a target the network can exploit: the reference operator is linear and the network is not. It does **not** establish that this is the cause of the A0.2 failures; the controls run so far (I1, I2) did not discriminate all alternative explanations (optimisation, scaling of the inputs, capacity of the head, propagation weights). It is a plausible explanation compatible with every outcome so far, and an interpretive limit to be stated.
- It says nothing about the real-data results of E9/E11, which are frozen and signed. It describes a property of the design under which those results were obtained; **whatever is decided below, it has to be reported in the paper next to the E9/E11 results**, as a measured structural property with the hop-distance and mass tables per city and seed in the appendix, and the interpretation of the G0/G1 contrast at f = 0.20 has to take it into account.

## 4. Options for an unresolved investigation (none recommended here)

Each option is to be read against the whole of P4R, not only E13: the campaign also plans E14 (regression track with G0-reg/G1-reg and the calendar baseline, A2), the 2×2 of the dedicated model (A3), E14-pre, and the replication on NDW-Rotterdam (E15, A4). I3 concerns the spatial path of `p4b.GWN`; it establishes nothing about the dedicated model (MoGERNN), whose propagation differs and would have to be measured on its own. The family of 24 pre-registered comparisons, the budgets and the Holm correction depend on which comparisons remain.

**A — Close E13 without A1.** The decision is a decision on the continuation of the campaign, not a diagnosis of impossibility: the synthetic positive control was not passed, the cause is unresolved, and I3 is documented as an interpretive limit. E13 is closed with a dated note; P4R is amended by a dated note recording the closure and its reason. What remains: E14's G0-reg/G1-reg uses the same GWN spatial path, so its G1 comparison inherits the same limit and should be reconsidered (kept with the limit stated, or dropped); the calendar baseline, the 2×2 of the dedicated model and the replication are not affected by I3 as such, but the dedicated model's spatial path has to be measured before relying on them; the revised family of comparisons and its correction are defined and pre-registered before any new result, with the originally planned and not executed comparisons kept on record. Cost: none in compute for E13; the campaign budget is re-planned.

**B — Revise: a pre-registered variant of the spatial path, separated from the historical pipeline.** A variant whose propagation reaches masked targets (for example a receptive field sized to the measured hop distances, propagation normalised by reach as in the R2 feature, or an adjacency over the k nearest observed nodes) can be an adaptation of existing methods rather than a new algorithmic contribution; if targets, masks and information availability are kept, the scientific question is preserved. It requires a new protocol (not an amendment of E13), with its own positive control, review, signature and deposit, and the structural measurement repeated for the new path; depth and adjacency change time and memory, so E13-pre-style costs must be measured before any fit — the A1 budget is not guaranteed. A positive result would be about the variant, not about the historical pipeline; the paper would report both.

**C — A different coverage scenario (per-node masking).** Per-node masking may increase the availability of observed neighbours at one hop, but it does not guarantee it: it depends on the graph and on the sampling and has to be measured as I3 did. It is a different coverage scenario, possibly complementary to area masking, not a directly comparable substitute; it changes what is being extrapolated and would not be compared with the frozen results as the same quantity.

In every option: A1 stays blocked until the co-authors decide; nothing further is trained before the decision is recorded as a dated note in P4R (and in E13 for A or B); the decision is taken by the co-authors with the internal reviewer, not by the coordination role.
