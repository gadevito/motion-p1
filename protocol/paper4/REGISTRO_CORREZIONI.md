# Register of corrections (Paper 4)

Consolidated list of the defects found during Paper 4, with the correction adopted and its source. Items 1–3
are defects of the deliverable UC-04 pipeline found during Phase 0 and the drafting of the protocol; they are
corrected in the Paper 4 pipeline, not in the deliverable. Item 7 is a defect of the deliverable's negative control, found while drafting E11; item 8 is a defect of the Paper 4 pipeline (the E11 generator), corrected before any output. Items 9–13 are defects of the Paper 4 pipeline, records or measurements found during Part B; none changed a number accepted by the internal review (register completed on 2026-11-08, after Part B was closed). Items 14–17 are defects of the deliverable found while drafting the pre-registration of Part A (`protocol/paper4/P4A.md`). They have no effect on the numbers of the paper, which are all regenerated. Items 18–20 are discrepancies between the deliverable's printed tables and the regenerated ones, found by the comparison in `CONGELAMENTO_4A.md` (a0b76dc). In each case the conserved script, run on the fingerprinted inputs, prints the regenerated values (`paper4/runs/p4a/script_*.txt`), so the deliverable's tables came from another version of the script or of its inputs. Their causes in the code of the deliverable are being traced by the executor; no number of the paper changes. Items 4–6 are defects of traceability of the
deliverable found in Phase 1 (questions D7–D9 of `paper4/DOMANDE.md`): register only, no Paper 4 number depends on them. The paper refers to the items by number.

Other defects of the deliverable found in Phase 0 are listed in `paper4/RICOGNIZIONE.md` §0, but are not
registered here because no Paper 4 number depends on them:

- the randomisation preserved only the edge count;
- hidden nodes entered the loss;
- the resampling used `np.isin`.
