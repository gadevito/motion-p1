# Register of corrections (Paper 4)

Consolidated list of the defects found during Paper 4, with the correction adopted and its source. Items 1–3
are defects of the deliverable UC-04 pipeline found during Phase 0 and the drafting of the protocol; they are
corrected in the Paper 4 pipeline, not in the deliverable. Item 7 is a defect of the deliverable's negative control, found while drafting E11; item 8 is a defect of the Paper 4 pipeline (the E11 generator), corrected before any output. Items 9–13 are defects of the Paper 4 pipeline, records or measurements found during Part B; none changed a number accepted by the internal review (register completed on 2026-11-08, after Part B was closed). Items 4–6 are defects of traceability of the
deliverable found in Phase 1 (questions D7–D9 of `paper4/DOMANDE.md`): register only, no Paper 4 number depends on them. The paper refers to the items by number.

Other defects of the deliverable found in Phase 0 are listed in `paper4/RICOGNIZIONE.md` §0, but are not
registered here because no Paper 4 number depends on them:

- the randomisation preserved only the edge count;
- hidden nodes entered the loss;
- the resampling used `np.isin`.
