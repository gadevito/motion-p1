# Register of corrections (Paper 4)

Consolidated list of the defects found during Paper 4, with the correction adopted and its source. Items 1–3
are defects of the deliverable UC-04 pipeline found during Phase 0 and the drafting of the protocol; they are
corrected in the Paper 4 pipeline, not in the deliverable. Item 7 is a defect of the deliverable's negative control, found while drafting E11; item 8 is a defect of the Paper 4 pipeline (the E11 generator), corrected before any output. Items 9–13 are defects of the Paper 4 pipeline, records or measurements found during Part B; none changed a number accepted by the internal review (register completed on 2026-11-08, after Part B was closed). Items 14–17 are defects of the deliverable found while drafting the pre-registration of Part A (`protocol/paper4/P4A.md`). They have no effect on the numbers of the paper, which are all regenerated. Items 18–20 are discrepancies between the deliverable's printed tables and the regenerated ones, found by the comparison in `CONGELAMENTO_4A.md` (a0b76dc). In each case the conserved script, run on the fingerprinted inputs, prints the regenerated values (`paper4/runs/p4a/script_*.txt`), so the deliverable's tables came from another version of the script or of its inputs. Their causes were measured by the executor, read-only, with the deliverable's conserved module (`analisi/p4a_cause.py`, `protocol/paper4/fase2/CAUSE_4A.md`, commit 03fd226): one is found (item 18, green class), the rest have no conserved generator. No number of the paper changes; the frozen record carries a dated note at its end. Item 21 is a design choice of the Paper 4 pipeline, declared on 2026-11-08 at the internal review of the Zenodo partition; it has no effect on the numbers. Item 22 is a defect of the design of E12-ter (an asymmetric fallback), measured by E12-quater on 2026-11-09; the frozen record of E12-ter is unchanged and the paper reports the measurement. Item 23 is a defect of the scoring of E10-pred (class weights of each design), found by the internal review of 2026-11-09 and corrected by E10-pred-bis from the saved predictions; the frozen record of E10-pred is unchanged and the paper reports the corrected comparison. Items 4–6 are defects of traceability of the
deliverable found in Phase 1 (questions D7–D9 of `paper4/DOMANDE.md`): register only, no Paper 4 number depends on them. The paper refers to the items by number.

Other defects of the deliverable found in Phase 0 are listed in `paper4/RICOGNIZIONE.md` §0, but are not
registered here because no Paper 4 number depends on them:

- the randomisation preserved only the edge count;
- hidden nodes entered the loss;
- the resampling used `np.isin`.
