## Question

E13 could not start A1: its synthetic positive control was not passed twice, and the investigations I1–I3 measured that, in the frozen E9/E11 design at f = 20 % (whole-cell masking, the kernel adjacency on the aligned road graph, the 6-step receptive field of the predictions of `p4b.GWN`), a consistent share of the nodes to predict has no path from an observed node within six steps and many others receive a small mass in the reference propagation operator; the cause of the failures is not established. E13B asks, on the same cities, node sets, masks and horizons as E9 at f = 20 %:

**(B1)** When the observed sites' readings and their derivatives are made available to the trained pipeline through a neighbourhood feature normalised by the propagated mass (and through the shared training statistics), beyond the shared calendar and structural information, does the prediction of the hidden sites improve — V(D1) against D0, same architecture, same channel count, same static information?

B0 asks, before any of this, whether the pipeline can be trusted to answer: it must exploit a dependence that is there, must not exploit one that is not, and must not see the hidden nodes anywhere.

## The variant (from `VARIANT_SPEC_E13B.md`, binding)

**GF.** `p4b.GWN` with `graph=False` (no graph convolution; start convolution, four blocks with temporal convolutions, skip branches, two output convolutions; hidden 32; dropout per configuration), inputs = the 11 channels of `p4b.Setup.batch` plus two channels computed for every node and every step of the window **from the input nodes of the batch only** (at validation and test, the observed nodes): **F**, the normalised neighbourhood factor, F_n(t) = Σ_m M[n,m]·om_m·r_m(t) / Σ_m M[n,m]·om_m where M = Σ_{k=1..6} A^k, A the kernel adjacency of G1 (`p4b.kernel_adjacency`), r the factor channel as in channel 0 (`Rf` times the input indicator), and F = 0 where the denominator is 0; **R**, the reach indicator, 1 where the denominator is > 0, else 0, constant over the window. Numerator and denominator in double precision on the CPU from M (float64), the input indicator and the window of r, the feature then converted to single precision (the frozen R2 computation of I2, verified against `tabular_diagnostic` within 6·10⁻⁸); any numerical optimisation is a separate change to be verified on its own. Unreachable nodes: F = 0, R = 0; their share is reported per (city, seed) for validation-hidden, test-hidden and sampled training targets (20 masks per split). No propagation beyond 6 steps, no kNN graph, no distance neighbourhood. `static=False` (the max_speed channel always zero); regime **I0 only**; 13 channels in both arms.

**Arms.** **D1** — the 13 channels. **D0** — same architecture, channels, static information, calendar, om and R; channels 0 (r), 1 (flow), 2 (occupancy), 3 (historical average) and F set to zero for every node and step. The primary contrast measures the benefit of making observed-site readings and their derivatives available to the trained pipeline, beyond the shared calendar and structural information; it does not isolate F alone (`BatchNorm2d` shares statistics across nodes during training; D0 removes recent readings and climatology together). D0 is a learned calendar-and-structure predictor on the classification task; it is not compared with the calendar baseline of E14, which predicts a continuous quantity (the comparison is not defined). No comparison with the historical G0 of E13 (its real fits do not exist). Both arms receive the same batches, masks, origins, seeds and initialisation streams.

| Factor | Levels | Note |
|---|---|---|

**Version binding of the records.** Every development and final record carries the frozen tag and the fingerprints of code and protocol at the time of the run; a record of another frozen version is kept on disk but is never reused: `latest_dev`/`latest_final` ignore it, the adequacy report lists the affected cells (`records_of_another_version`) and the lock is False until they are re-run under the current version. **Nested tuning per split, final trajectory, adequacy rule and extension, global lock, one development run per cell:** exactly as E13 (§Design of `E13.md`, binding here with "arm ∈ {D1, D0}", "regime = I0" and the cap, configurations C1–C4, checkpoints every 500, selection rule, development stream 7 and final stream 6, extension to 20 000 with reproduction within 1e-9, lock file `e13b_adequacy.json`, `require_signed` on this protocol). **Perimeter unchanged from E9/E11/E13:** per-batch masks among observed cells only, u ~ U(0.6, 0.95); hidden nodes never in the training loss; their **own readings** never in the inputs and never in the preprocessing statistics (normalisation, historical average, class weights) — calendar, om, F and R are admitted inputs on hidden nodes, F and R being computed from input nodes only, and BatchNorm involves every node of the batch; validation-hidden nodes serve the authorised selection; admissible origins, split 70/10/20 with purge; six random streams plus stream 7; all arms of a (city, s) on the same machine (the Mac, MPS).

**What differs from E13, declared:** arms D1/D0 in place of G0/G1 (no graph convolution in either arm; the neighbourhood enters as an input feature); regime I0 only; 30 cells and 150 trainings in place of 60 and 300; the two feature channels; the budget of E13 is not transferred — the cost is measured by E13B-pre and recorded in the signature line.

## Unit of analysis and inference (P4R §7, as E13)

Cell = (city, seed, corridor block, node, origin). wCE on test-hidden nodes per horizon, mean over the four horizons. V_c = mean over seeds of 100·(wCE(D0) − wCE(D1))/wCE(D0), in points of the D0 wCE; a paired difference on identical targets, origins and masks. Resampling, p-values and simultaneous bounds exactly as E13 (two-way block bootstrap with multiplicities shared across splits and arms, 4 000 replicates, generator `default_rng([20261119, city index])`, 95 % percentile intervals; centred bootstrap p-values; sup-t band over the three contrasts). **Undefined contrasts (fixed now):** a split whose subset of test-hidden nodes receives no weight in a replicate has an undefined contrast, and the replicate is undefined as a whole (never averaged across the undefined split); intervals and p-values are computed from the finite replicates only, and a contrast is reported as *undefined*, with no p-value, if its observed value is undefined or if fewer than 95 % of its replicates are finite; the sup-t band uses the replicates defined for every city and is undefined otherwise. The number of undefined replicates is reported. The seed-to-seed spread is reported next to the interval. Cells and nodes without valid targets are excluded with their counts; the share of unreachable test-hidden nodes per (city, seed) is reported next to every contrast.

## Criteria

**Primary (three contrasts, one per city).** V_c(D1) with its interval. The family-wise correction belongs to the campaign-wide analysis over the revised P4R family (to be pre-registered in the P4R revision before any new result); `e13b.py analysis` reports the three with marginal intervals and unadjusted p-values and its statement is **always labelled PROVISIONAL**. Readings on the two scales of E11 (sign; margin of 3 points). The statement is derived mechanically:

| Statement | Condition |
|---|---|
| "observed-site readings, delivered through the normalised neighbourhood feature, help at 20 % under adequate training" | **(a)** evidence of benefit: the one-sided positive test of V_c(D1) > 0 is significant after the correction of the pre-registered family (P4R revision) in at least 2 cities; **(b)** size: the point estimate of V_c(D1) is ≥ 3 in those cities — reported as an estimate, distinct from a demonstration that the benefit is at least 3, which would require a lower bound with the correct coverage for the family of statements made (the simultaneous band of the pre-registered family, not a marginal interval chosen after the results) to be ≥ 3, and is stated separately if it holds; **(c)** adequacy: every cell entering the cited contrasts passes the final-fit adequacy diagnostics |
| "gain below the prespecified 3-point margin at 20 % under adequate training" | the simultaneous upper bound of V_c(D1) over the three cities is < 3 (a measurable gain smaller than the margin may exist and is reported as estimated), **and** every cell entering the three contrasts passes the final-fit adequacy diagnostics |
| "undetermined" | every other case, reported with counts |

"Under adequate training" requires condition (c) for both conclusions. Before the P4R revision fixes the family and its correction, no statement other than "PROVISIONAL" is produced.

Neither statement is a statement about G1 or about graph convolutions: D1 and D0 share the architecture and differ in the availability of the readings and their derivatives.

**Secondary, declared.** (i) **Reachable versus unreachable test-hidden nodes:** V_c(D1) computed separately on the test-hidden nodes with R = 1 and with R = 0, descriptive, with intervals from the same replicates; the R = 0 nodes receive no neighbourhood information by construction, so an effect there describes the difference between the two trained pipelines on those nodes and does not isolate the contribution of the shared training statistics. (ii) **Selected configurations and checkpoints**, tabulated per cell, with the validation curves in the appendix. No comparison of D0 with the calendar baseline of E14: D0 yields class probabilities assessed by wCE, that baseline predicts a continuous quantity assessed by MAE/RMSE, and the comparison is not defined.

**Reported without a criterion:** absolute wCE and distance from ln 3 per cell; red recall at 5 % false alarms; every quantity per horizon; the share of unreachable nodes per (city, seed) and set.
