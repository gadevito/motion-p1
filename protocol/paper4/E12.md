## Question

E9 found that a learned propagation (G1) gives no measurable gain over the model without spatial exchange (G0),
at any coverage from 10 to 60% and in any of the three cities. That leaves two readings:

1. the hidden segments carry no spatial signal that their instrumented neighbours could pass on, at these
   distances and with these data;
2. the signal exists, but the graph models do not learn to use it in the inductive regime.

E12 separates the two without training anything. It asks whether simple spatial predictors, built only from
the observed segments, beat G0 on the same hidden segments, origins and node sets as E9.

## Design

**Perimeter: identical to E9.**

- the three cities, *f* ∈ {10, 20, 40, 60}%, seeds 0–4;
- node sets from the signed E9 rule (grid 11/11/15, deficit on (*f*, 20%, 80% − *f*));
- admissible origins, split 70/10/20 with purge;
- test-hidden segments and test origins as in E9.

The node sets are rebuilt with `p4b.Setup` and checked against the fingerprints stored in the E9 run records.
The reference G0 is the E9 run of the same (city, *f*, seed), read from its per-cell file.

**Information available to every predictor.** At origin *t*, a predictor may use:

- the classes of the **observed** segments at times ≤ *t*;
- statistics of the observed segments over the **training period**;
- UTM distances between segments.

It never uses a hidden segment's data, a value after *t*, or anything from the validation or test periods
except the observed segments' values up to *t*.

**Predictors.** Each produces, for each hidden segment, origin and horizon *h* ∈ {15, 30, 60, 120 min}, a
class distribution *q*.

| Name | Rule |
|---|---|
| **P-nn** | class at *t* of the nearest observed segment with a valid class at *t*, persisted to *t* + *h* (*q* = one-hot) |
| **P-idw4** | inverse-distance-weighted (1/*d*²) average of the one-hot classes at *t* of the 4 nearest observed segments with a valid class at *t* |
| **P-idw500** | the same over all observed segments within 500 m with a valid class at *t*; if none, the prior (counted) |
| **P-hist4** | 1/*d*²-weighted average, over the 4 nearest observed segments, of their class frequencies in the weekly slot of *t* + *h* over the training period |
| **Prior** (reference) | class frequencies of the observed segments over the training period |

**From *q* to a probability.** The transformation that minimises the wCE for a given class estimate *q* is
applied identically to every predictor, with no fitted parameter:

1. *q*′ = (1 − ε)·*q* + ε·prior, with ε = 0.05 (the "hard ε" of the deliverable's interpolation references,
   inherited); this keeps every class probability above zero;
2. *p* ∝ W·*q*′, with W the class weights of the run (the deliverable's `--calib-mode weighted`).

No parameter is fitted on validation or test. **Secondary column:** the wCE of *q*′ without the reweighting,
so that the reader sees what the transformation alone is worth. Approved by the internal review on 2026-11-08
(10:40).

## Metrics, unit and resample

- wCE on test-hidden segments: per horizon, Σ W[y]·(−log p_y) / Σ W[y] over valid cells, then mean over the
  four horizons; cells without a valid target are excluded with their count.
- **V(P) = mean over seeds of 100·(wCE(G0) − wCE(P))/wCE(G0)**, in points of the G0 wCE, positive when P is
  better than G0. G0 is the E9 run of the same (city, *f*, seed), so V is paired by seed and segment.
- Red recall on test-hidden segments at 5% false alarms (threshold chosen on validation-hidden segments for
  each predictor), next to the realised false-alarm rate.
- Intervals: the E9 procedure, 4,000 replicates, seeds × corridor blocks (grid cells) holding test-hidden
  segments, multiplicities kept, the same replicate indices for every predictor and for G0 of a (city, *f*).
  Generator `default_rng([20261106, index of (city, f)])`, as in E9, so the replicates coincide with E9's.
- Reading scales and margin of 3 points as in E9.

## Criterion, with threshold

**"Spatial signal present"** if at least one predictor among P-nn, P-idw4, P-idw500 and P-hist4 has V(P)
favourable (L > 0) and V(P) ≥ 3 points in at least 2 of the 3 cities at some level *f*.

**Derived statement:**

| Statement | Condition |
|---|---|
| **"a spatial signal is present, recoverable by P at f"** | the criterion holds; P and the levels at which it holds are named |
| **"no spatial signal recoverable by these predictors at 10–60% coverage"** | for every predictor, city and *f*, V(P) has U < 3 |
| **"undetermined"** | every other case, reported with the counts |

The Prior is reported as the floor: V(Prior) shows how far G0 itself is from a class-frequency prediction. It
does not enter the criterion. Under the reweighting the Prior becomes exactly the uniform predictor (wCE = ln 3),
because the class weights are the inverse of the same class frequencies (W·π is constant). The unweighted
column (*q*′ = prior) is the class-frequency prediction proper. This is a property of the transformation,
declared here (found by the self-test, 2026-11-08).

There is no correction for the 4 predictors × 12 (city, *f*) comparisons. The protection is the 3-point
threshold and the two-city requirement, declared here and in the paper.

**Reported without a criterion:**

- **spatial concordance of the labels themselves**, computed before and independently of any predictor. For
  each (city, *f*), pooled over the five seeds and the test origins:
  - the share of pairs (test-hidden segment, its nearest observed segment) with the same class at the same
    instant, among pairs where both classes are valid;
  - next to the agreement expected from the prior of the observed segments, Σ_k π_k²;
  - by distance between the two segments: 0–250, 250–500, 500–1,000 and > 1,000 m.

  It says whether the labels carry a spatial signal before any predictor is applied.
- V(P) − V(G1 of E9), per (city, *f*): whether a non-learned predictor does better than the learned
  propagation;
- the wCE of every predictor without the reweighting (*q*′ only), secondary column;
- red recall at 5% false alarms with the realised rate;
- share of hidden segments with no observed segment within 500 m, per (city, *f*);
- the covariates of E9 (cell side, median distance test-hidden → observed);
- per-horizon wCE, and **V(P) per horizon** (15, 30, 60 and 120 min), with intervals from the same replicates:
  a spatial signal, if present, should show at 15 min and fade by 120 min.

## Mandatory controls (preflight, reading no test target)

1. Node sets, admissible origins and class weights rebuilt for every (city, *f*, seed) and identical to the
   fingerprints of the E9 run records.
2. Causality: noise on every value after *t*, and on every hidden segment at any time, leaves every predictor's
   *q* at origin *t* identical to the bit.
3. Training-period statistics (prior, weekly-slot frequencies) are computed from observed segments only; an
   independent recomputation matches.
4. The E9 G0 per-cell files are found for all 60 (city, *f*, seed) pairs, with fingerprints matching their
   run.json.
5. Every *p* is a probability (finite, non-negative, sums to 1 within 1e-6) on every target cell of a sample of
   origins.
6. Synthetic test with a known effect, as in the E9 generator: a planted spatial signal must be recovered with
   the expected sign and size.
7. The neighbour lists of every hidden segment contain observed segments only; no hidden segment appears as a
   neighbour, as a source of a statistic or in the prior.
8. No fitted parameter: the only quantities computed from data are the prior and the weekly-slot frequencies,
   both from the training period of the observed segments (control 3).
9. The red-recall threshold of every predictor is chosen on the validation-hidden segments and saved with its
   fingerprint before any test metric is computed, as in E9. The run refuses to compute a test metric if the
   threshold file is missing or its fingerprint differs.

## What invalidates the experiment

- a predictor, ε, the neighbour rule or the 500 m radius changed after any test number;
- any parameter fitted on test;
- node sets or origins different from E9's;
- any use of hidden-segment data;
- a G0 other than the E9 run of the same (city, *f*, seed);
- a number of the frozen record written by hand.

## Cost

No training. One pass over the test origins of 60 (city, *f*, seed) pairs and five predictors, on CPU. To be
measured in the preflight (isolated) and written in the signature line.

## What changes in the paper

- **"Spatial signal present":** the data carry a neighbour signal that the learned propagation does not use in
  the inductive regime. The paper states this as a limit of the models, and placement questions remain open.
- **"No spatial signal":** at these coverages and distances the instrumented neighbours carry nothing the hidden
  segments can use. "Placement cannot buy accuracy" then holds for learned and non-learned predictors alike,
  and closes Part B.
- **"Undetermined":** reported with the counts; the paper does not claim either.
