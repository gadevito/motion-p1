## Why

E12-sexies set R×(*h*), normalised by the neighbour's excess agreement at *h* = 0, beside R_own(*h*), normalised by
the segment's agreement with itself at *h* = 0, which is 1. The two ratios have different denominators, so 0.60
against 0.27 does not compare the two on one scale, and each was computed on its own valid cells. E12-sexies-bis
reports the two excesses unnormalised, on the same cells.

## Design

**Perimeter, pairs, slots and replicates: identical to E12-sexies.** Three cities, *f* ∈ {10, 20, 40, 60}%, seeds
0–4; test-hidden segment *i*, nearest observed segment *j* (static), test origins *t*; lags *h* ∈ {0, 15, 30, 60,
120} min; hour-of-week slots with π_k(s) from the observed segments over the training period (N_min = 20); E12's
replicate indices, the same draws for every *h*; distance bins as E12.

**Cells.** For each lag, the cells (*i*, *t*) where *y_j*(*t*), *y_i*(*t*) and *y_i*(*t* + *h*) are all valid: the
neighbour's and the segment's own agreement are computed on the same cells.

**Quantities, per (city, *f*, bin, *h*), on those cells.**

| Symbol | Definition |
|---|---|
| A(*h*) | share with *y_i*(*t* + *h*) = *y_j*(*t*) |
| P(*h*) | share with *y_i*(*t* + *h*) = *y_i*(*t*) |
| E×(*h*) | Σ_k π_k(s_t)·π_k(s_{t+h}), the agreement of two independent draws, each from the calendar of its own time; the same reference for both, since both compare a class at *t* with the class of *i* at *t* + *h* |
| X_nb(*h*) | A(*h*) − E×(*h*): the neighbour's excess agreement, in points |
| X_own(*h*) | P(*h*) − E×(*h*): the segment's own excess agreement, in points |
| X_nb(*h*) − X_own(*h*) | = A(*h*) − P(*h*), paired within each replicate |

At *h* = 0, P = 1 and X_own(0) = 1 − E×(0). Reported with 95% intervals within 250 m (Madrid and London; Melbourne
without intervals where the bin has fewer than 3 blocks in some seed), and for the other bins in the record.
**No reading rule**: the paper states the values; any interpretation of a common component or of propagation stays
a hypothesis.

**Before the signature** (preflight, seed 0, validation origins, no target read): requiring the three classes
valid keeps, at 15 minutes, 89–93% of the cells of *h* = 0 in Madrid, 78–80% in London and 81–84% in Melbourne;
from 15 to 120 minutes the count falls by a further 1–4%.

## Mandatory controls (preflight, reading no test target)

1. Node sets, origins and pairs identical to E9 and E12 (fingerprints); E12-sexies run records present.
2. At *h* = 0, on validation origins, the cells are E12's pairs, A(0) equals E12's agreement and E×(0) equals
   E12-sexies' E×(0), to the bit, per segment; P(0) = 1.
3. For *h* > 0, on validation origins, the cells are exactly those where the neighbour pair and the own pair of
   E12-sexies are both valid, and their counts are not above either.
4. π_k(s) from observed segments and training-period cells only: noise on hidden segments and after the last
   training cell leaves it identical.
5. Synthetic: in a world where the neighbour has no memory beyond the calendar, X_nb(15) is close to 0; where it is
   persistent, X_nb(15) is large; X_own(0) = 1 − E×(0) exactly.
6. Test-period computation refused while the signature line is blank.
