# Register of corrections (English translation)

Corrections made during the study: to the pipeline (entries 1–16, 17a, 19–27, 29), to the
documentation (28), to the repository history (30) and to the manuscript (17b, 18, 31). **This file is a translation of `protocol/REGISTRO_CORREZIONI.md`, which is the record**;
where the two differ, the Italian file prevails. The paper refers to these entries by number.

Column "Source": the protocol or frozen record that carries the dated correction; for entries with no
file in this repository, "supplement table up to `manoscritto-20261015f`; `NOTE_PER_GLI_AUTORI.md`"
(the table of the manuscript's supplement, present up to tag `manoscritto-20261015f` and removed in
`manoscritto-20261015g`; the authors' notes are in the manuscript repository, not in this one).

How they were found: 1–16 by assertions, preflight checks or fingerprint comparisons; 17 by reading a
frozen record against the manuscript; 19 and 20 by the regeneration script (`analysis/main.py`); 21
while describing the network from the checkpoint weights; 22 by regeneration on a second machine; 23
while generating a supplementary table from the class-weight files actually loaded; 24 by a control of
E4 during the sensitivity experiment; 25 by regeneration from scratch; 26 by reading the E14 record
against its own tables; 27 in the second external review; none by inspecting the code for its own sake.
Entries 17b and 18 are errors of the manuscript, not of the pipeline.
